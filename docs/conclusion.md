This lab demonstrates how WxCC leverages autonomous AI agents, API integrations, and developer tooling to modernize modern customer service operations. Across six hands-on labs, participants gain end-to-end expertise in:

* **Building Autonomous AI Agents:** Setting up self-learning agents, managing knowledge repositories, and connecting voice and chat channels for seamless customer resolution.


* **Extending Platform Capabilities:** Leveraging Webex Connect flows and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to integrate external APIs, order management systems, and backend operational tools directly into the AI agent environment.


* **Enhancing Human & AI Collaboration:** Utilizing real-time conversation summaries for smooth call escalations and applying AI-driven Quality Management tools to identify coaching needs and raise customer satisfaction.


* **Streamlining Operations & Developer Workflows:** Empowering system architects and administrators to use natural language queries and AI clients for real-time telemetry analysis, troubleshooting, and draft flow updates.
